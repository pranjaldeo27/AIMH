The judge

claude-sonnet-5 — tactic_analysis.py --tag

A sixth model, playing a different role: it doesn't answer the vignette, it grades the five answers against a 12-tactic codebook (borrowed from a paper on "templatic" AI empathy), then compresses each response into a short tag sequence and writes it to the third sheet tab.

Stigma scoring

analysis.py

Do the five models score willingness differently? Kruskal–Wallis tests, inter-model correlation, mean score by crisis type — rendered as bar charts, pooled and per crisis type.
